Map of Kijani

Where is everything in the hidden village? This is one imagined map of Kijani — the old stone temple at the centre, Oya's hut beside it, Amara's healing hut by the river, the hunters' shelters up in the trees, and the training ground at the edge of the clearing, all ringed by thick jungle. It is a picture made for fun, not a book illustration: the book has no map in it, so this is just one way to picture the village.
